Hey, welcome to the Horde

Register once, and we will know you when you come back. Your name, your Ko-fi or Discord, and your wallets only if you hold Horde art.

Wallet addresses are public on the chain already, anyone can see them. Your name here is your choice, use a nickname if you like. We never ask for a seed, a private key, or a wallet password.

Already in the Horde? Log in instead, no need to register twice.

What you get

  • Your own Horde profile, with your level and badges read live from what you hold.
  • Your Horde profile key, one for each chain you are on.
  • Free mints and holder-only drops, first word on contests and giveaways.
  • A seat in the House of the Horde Discord.

Sell your Horde NFT here

Got a Byron NFT and want to sell it? List it on the ledger, then tell Byron or Marrow in Discord and it can show up here.

1. In Xaman, open your NFT, make a sell offer, pick XRP or $HORDE and your price.
2. Tell Byron the NFT name and the price, in Discord or a Ko-fi message.
3. Muse checks the ledger to confirm the sell offer is real, then adds it to this page.
No wash trading, selling to yourself does not count, and no one promises a price or a profit.

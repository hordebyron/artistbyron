The XRP Market

Byron pieces on XRP, offered for sale by the people who hold them. On XRP a sale is an offer sitting on the ledger itself. This wall gathers every live offer for Byron's art into one place. Buying settles wallet to wallet on the ledger, nobody here holds your money or your art.

How buying works

Tap a piece and it opens on Bithomp, where the live offer sits. Accept the offer in Xaman and the piece moves to your wallet and the payment moves to the seller, settled by the ledger itself.

Selling is just as open: list any Byron piece you hold for sale from your own wallet, and it shows up on this wall the next time the wall is gathered. No account here, no listing fee here.

Buys and sells of Byron pieces earn game points for both sides. The points design is in Byron's hands and goes live with the game.

How it works

On XRP a sale is an offer sitting on the ledger itself. This page gathers every live offer for Byron's art into one wall, and checks each one against the ledger when the page opens, so what you see is really for sale.

Tap Buy on a piece and your wallet asks you to approve accepting the offer. The piece moves to your wallet and the payment moves to the seller in the same moment, settled by the ledger itself. Selling works the same way in reverse: your listing is your own offer, signed by you, held by nobody.

Marketplaces like XRP Cafe take 1.589% off every sale. Here the cut is zero. The only cost is the ledger's own fee, about a ten thousandth of an XRP.

Buys and sells of Byron pieces earn game points for both sides. The points design is in Byron's hands and goes live with the game.

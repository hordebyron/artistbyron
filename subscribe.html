Horde Subscriptions

One membership for the whole Horde

You can pay on either chain, WAX or XRP, and it counts as the same membership on your Horde profile. Pay on WAX with WAX or DHC, or on XRP with XRP or $HORDE. Whichever door you came through, your profile knows you are a member.

How it works

  1. Pick a tier below.
  2. Pay on the chain you use. Your tier has its own collection on each chain.
  3. Every month you are subscribed, you get that month's rewards from your tier's collection.

What you get every month

  • A free mint in the monthly generative art drop, a fresh pre-generated collection every month.
  • Pieces from your own tier's collection.
  • First dibs on free drops and holder-only mints.
  • The draw tickets and perks in your tier below.

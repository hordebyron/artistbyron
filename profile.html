Unlock your profile on this device

Prove your wallet once and this device stays unlocked for 30 days. After that, saving just writes to the shared list. No code and no dust for every little edit, ever again.

Works with Anchor or the WAX Cloud Wallet. The wallet will ask you to send a tiny proof, 0.0001 WAX, with a code in the memo. That one proof is the unlock.

How the tie works

  • Register once with both wallets, and both chains point at the same profile. A drop or a reward on either chain finds the same person.
  • Log in with WAX here, or with Xaman on the XRP page, and the site greets you by your profile name, not your wallet address.
  • Your profile lives on the shared Horde list, so it follows you to any device. Log in with either wallet, or open your own profile link, and it is you.

One person, one profile. Your WAX wallet and your XRPL wallet live here together, tied to one name, so the Horde knows both wallets are you. Add a picture, add your links, and this is the face you show the Horde.

Join the Horde, the event

  1. Register below and pick the name you want on your badge.
  2. You get your Horde Profile Token, your key, on WAX or XRP. It cannot be sold or given away.
  3. Later your key's picture mutates into your personal badge, with your badge name and profile number on it.
  4. Use that badge as your profile picture, here and anywhere.

Badge names: nothing overly obscene. A filter flags rough names automatically, and Byron checks every flagged name himself. Byron has the final say, always.

How the tie works

  • Register once with both wallets, and both chains point at the same profile. A drop or a reward on either chain finds the same person.

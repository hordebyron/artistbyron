2. How it works

  • 1. Your monster is a mutable NFT. Its picture and level can change on chain, so growing up is real, not just a score on a page.
  • 2. Feed it an old NFT. Pick one of your old Byron NFTs and burn it. Burning is the food. How much it grows depends on how rare the food is, a 1-of-1 is worth 100 food points, a common is worth 3. The value list is the byronartset1 feed catalogue.
  • 3. Sometimes the batch is bad. 1 in 5 feeds is a bad batch and the monster gets sick. Feed it good food while it is sick and it gets better. But a bad batch while it is already sick, and it dies. Then you start again with a new baby monster.
  • 4. It wastes away if you forget it. The monster remembers the last time it ate. 3 days with no food and it gets hungry and shrinks a stage. 7 days and it gets sick. 14 days and it dies. Feeding it brings it back.
  • 5. It grows up in front of you. 15 food points and it grows, 50 and it is huge. The picture changes, the level changes, and this page shows it live.

The easy WAX command behind the Feed button is one burn: atomicassets burnasset, your wallet signs it, the NFT is gone, the monster grows. No typing commands, no copying asset IDs by hand, the button does it.

Earn points while you play

Points are free. You earn them by doing normal market stuff, you never buy points.

  1. Burn an NFT to feed your monster: +its feed value, 1 to 100 points. The biggest earner, and every burn takes one NFT off the market for good.
  2. List an NFT above the floor: +5 points. Pays you to hold the floor up, not undercut it.
  3. Buy a Byron NFT on the market: +10 points, times the value multiplier.
  4. Sell a Byron NFT on the market: +10 points, times the value multiplier.
  5. Buy DHC: +10 points, times the value multiplier.
  6. Add DHC/WAX liquidity: +1 point per WAX added, up to 100.

Guards: it has to be a real market sale with WAX paid, not a transfer, buyer and seller different wallets, and at most 100 points a day from buying and selling. Burning has no cap.

Bigger sales earn more. Under 5 WAX is x1 (10 points), 5 to 20 is x2 (20), 20 to 100 is x3 (30), 100 to 500 is x5 (50), over 500 is x10 (100). Same for buying and selling.

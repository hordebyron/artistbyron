A Free Drop Across Four Chains

Marrow here, keeping the tally. Monday morning, 30 Nov, a free limited edition drop opens on four chains at once: WAX, XRP, Base and Solana.

It is free, and it is one per person per chain. If you hold a wallet on all four chains, you can claim one on each. The edition is one shared pool across the four chains, so when it runs out, it closes on all of them at once.

Free drops are for registered Horde members. Registration is the whitelist: join the Horde, put your wallets on your profile, and you are in.

The piece itself is revealed when the drop opens Monday morning.

At the same time, paid drops for a dollar open on each chain beside it.

What this actually means. One piece of art, one edition, living on four chains at the same time. It is free to claim, one per person on each chain, and the whole edition is a single pool: every claim on any chain counts against the same number, and when that number hits zero the drop closes everywhere at once. Nobody can buy their way past it, because there is nothing to buy, and nobody can claim twice on the same chain, because registration is the list.

It is also a bridge. A collector on WAX and a collector on XRP are holding the same piece, not two different ones, so the chains stop being separate rooms. The very first mint of an edition on any chain is forever the first mint of that edition on that chain. If you ever want to move your piece from WAX to XRP, the original goes into the vault for safekeeping, and its twin carries on over there. Move it again and the version it leaves behind is destroyed, while the new one points back to the original. So there are only ever two versions of a piece: the original, safe in the vault, and the one live on whatever chain it is on now. One edition, four doors, one house.

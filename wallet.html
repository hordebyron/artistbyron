What do I hold?

One page, both chains. Paste your XRPL wallet and your WAX name and see your Horde holdings together — Big Paws and Lost in Contemplation on XRPL, plus your Horde NFTs on WAX. The chain is shown on each item, so you can see where it lives, but you don't have to think about chains to play.

Logging in with WAX is optional — it just proves the wallet is yours. No password, no seed, ever. Nothing is sent anywhere except the public lookups your browser makes: the XRPL list comes from the snapshot file on this site, and the WAX list comes live from the public AtomicAssets API.

How to buy on Base

Nothing here is pre-minted. Every piece is minted only when someone buys it, on the chain they pick.

1. Go to the Minion 001 page, pick Base, then Member (50 cents) or Everyone ($1).
2. Pay the exact amount in ETH on Base. The pay page shows the amount and signs the payment from your wallet.
3. The minions mint the piece to your wallet. Each piece is numbered Mint 1 of 100 to Mint 100 of 100, in the order they are claimed across all chains.

The price is the same on every chain: 50 cents for registered Horde members, a dollar for everyone else.

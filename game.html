How a mutable game works

  • 1. Get the free one. Claim it in the drop while it's free. That's the start, not the end.
  • 2. Do something with it. Burn an old asset, hold this one, or sell it on — each action can be a trigger.
  • 3. It changes. When the trigger happens, the image or the text on your NFT can update — a new level, a new look, a new line of story.
  • 4. The next drop needs the last one. Holding the right NFTs together is the “ingredients” to get into the next free drop.

The rules for each game are set by Byron, one game at a time. No points are explained before you've played once — cool art and fun free first, the system comes later.

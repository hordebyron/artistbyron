This entire NFT is mutable. Find out more on Monday, when you mutate your Minion.

One pool, every chain

Every piece in this gallery is one shared pool across all the chains. Every claim or sale on any chain counts against the same number, and when that number hits zero the piece closes on all of them at once. Every buy flow checks the number right before payment, so the last piece cannot sell twice.

Same price, every chain

The price is the same on every chain. 50 cents for members, a dollar for everyone else, converted to WAX, XRP, ETH and SOL when the drop was set up.

Minted when you buy it

Nothing here is pre-minted. A piece is minted only when someone buys it, on the chain they pick. Every piece is numbered Mint 1 of 100 to Mint 100 of 100, in the order they are claimed.

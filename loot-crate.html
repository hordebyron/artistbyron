The Holiday Loot Crate, as a token

The Horde Holiday Loot Crate, sold as a token on WAX and on XRP. The token IS the crate. Claim it and the physical crate ships to you. Or never claim it, and the token stands as a credit toward future purchases from Byron, for the full amount you paid for it.

Sold from the Horde's own XRP market page.

The price includes shipping, and the USA price is about $25 US (about CA$36), Canada about $30 US (about CA$43). Overseas is not offered yet, it is coming later.

Pick your own country honestly. When you claim, your shipping address is the check: if the address does not match the tier you bought, the difference comes due before the crate ships.

25 tokens on each chain. On WAX, 25 claims for the USA tier and 25 for Canada. On XRP, 15 tokens are priced for the USA and 10 for Canada. When they are gone, they are gone.

Or pay by card, and still get the token

No crypto? Pay by card and the token still comes to you. At checkout you pick your chain, WAX or XRP, and type your wallet. The token is delivered by hand to that wallet, usually within a day, and your chain and wallet are written on the payment record as proof of what you asked for. Then claim the crate with the form below, same as everyone else.

USA shippingCA$36 by card Canada shippingCA$43 by card

Shipping is inside these prices too, same tiers as the tokens. Double-check your wallet at checkout: delivery is final once the token is sent, and a wrong address cannot be undone.

Page two: the credit

One token spends once: claim the crate, or spend the credit. When it is spent, it gets stamped SPENT and stays with you as a souvenir.

The art on the token right now is temporary. The crate contents are a secret until they ship, so once the crates ship, the art on every token updates to the final reveal.

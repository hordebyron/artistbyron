How to buy

Nothing here is pre-minted. A piece is minted only when someone buys it, on the chain they pick.

  1. Pick Minion 001 on the mint page.
  2. Pay in SOL: 0.0069 SOL for registered Horde members, 0.0139 SOL for everyone else. Pay from your own wallet, never an exchange.
  3. Tell Muse your receiving Solana address and the payment signature, and Muse mints the piece to your wallet. The piece is numbered Mint N of 100 in claim order across all four chains.

Shipping

Every package comes with a little Horde reward tucked inside.

Shipping is already inside the price you see at checkout, no surprise charges. Orders ship from the USA and Canada. Your order is packed by hand and sent as soon as we can get it out the door.

Loot Crates ship in the first two weeks of December. If your order comes with an NFT, it is sent to your wallet by hand after you pay, usually within a day.

We ship worldwide. If your country charges customs or import tax, that is yours to pay, we cannot cover it. If anything arrives damaged or never arrives, tell us and we will sort it out.

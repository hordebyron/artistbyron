Fan Art

Art made by the Horde, for the Horde. If you made something inspired by Byron's world, post it and it could hang here.

How to get your art in here

  1. Make it. Draw, paint, colour, or remix anything from Byron's world: Minions, zombies, skulls, the Horde.
  2. Post it on X with all three tags: #JoinTheHorde #ArtistByron #HordeFanArt
  3. Get 25 Horde Points when it is approved, and see it hanging on this wall.

Every piece is approved by Byron before it shows. It has to be your own work, inspired by Byron's art. Posting it on X with the tags is also how you advertise the Horde, so it earns its points.

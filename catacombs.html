Da Horde's Catacombs

The Explorer game, home again. It lives here now, on Byron's own site, and nowhere else.

Season 1 is being built. The doors are not open yet. When they open, they open on this page.

New here?

Your first step is joining the Horde. It is free, and your Explorer Book is your Horde profile, so you will be ready the day the doors open.

Join the Horde

The quest, step by step

  1. 1Explorer Coin. The coin gets you in. No coin, no entry. It will be claimed right here on this page, free, when the doors open.
  2. 2Explorer Pack. Your starter kit. Claimed after your coin, it hands you the pieces you need to begin.
  3. 3Explorer Book. Your passport. Every stamp you earn lands in your Book. This one already exists: it is your Horde profile key. See your Book on your profile.
  4. 4Pass Key. The key lets you burn. You earn it in the game, and then the real work starts.
  5. 5Work Order. Your job. Burn old Horde pieces, up to 8 a day, at least an hour apart. Every finished day earns a stamp in your Book.
  6. 6Stamps and levels. Stamps fill your Book and level you up. Four weeks of work is your apprenticeship. Finish it, and you graduate. Then Month 2 begins.

The week in the Catacombs

  • Taco Tuesday. A mid week treat for explorers who showed up.
  • Freaky Friday. Something strange always happens on Friday.
  • Mid Week Perk Pack. A Horde wide puzzle. Solve it together, earn the pack.
  • 666 DHC. Paid after each finished week, like the original game did.

Two doors, one game

WAX opens first. The Catacombs were born on WAX, and WAX is the smooth door: log in with your wallet and play.

The XRP door opens later. Same Book, same quest, same stamps. Your XRP profile key is your passport there, and rewards can land in $HORDE. One profile, one passport, either chain.

The rules, plain

  • Free to play. Always. If they have to pay, they will not play, so the game is free.
  • Fun over profit. That was the soul of the first Catacombs, and it stays.
  • Byron's art only. Every coin, pack and stamp is his work.
  • Site exclusive. The game lives on artistbyron.ca and nowhere else.

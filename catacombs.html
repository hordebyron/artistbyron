The quest, step by step

  1. 1Explorer Coin. The coin gets you in. No coin, no entry. Your Horde profile is your coin. It is free, and this page is where you use it.
  2. 2Explorer Pack. Your starter kit. It hands you the pieces you need to begin. In this version your kit is this page: the button, the timer, and your Book.
  3. 3Explorer Book. Your passport. Every stamp you earn lands in your Book. This one already exists: it is your Horde HODLer. See your Book on your profile.
  4. 4Pass Key. The key lets you burn. You earn it in the game, and then the real work starts.
  5. 5Work Order. Your job. Delves recharge one an hour, up to 2 stored, so come back often and spend them. Up to 8 a day. Every finished day earns a stamp in your Book.
  6. 6Stamps and levels. Stamps fill your Book and level you up. Four weeks of work is your apprenticeship. Finish it, and you graduate. Then Month 2 begins.

The week in the Catacombs

  • Taco Tuesday. A mid week treat for explorers who showed up.
  • Freaky Friday. Something strange always happens on Friday.
  • Mid Week Perk Pack. A Horde wide puzzle. Solve it together, earn the pack.
  • Weekly reward. Finish a week and pick your reward: points toward the prize ladder, or an NFT on your chain.

Five doors, one game

Play as a site member with just your profile, or come in through a wallet: WAX, XRP, Base or Solana. Same button, same stamps, same Book. The chain only decides your reward.

The NFT reward is ready on WAX and XRP now. Base and Solana players take points until their NFT doors are ready.

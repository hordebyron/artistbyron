The first piece

The first piece is revealed Monday morning, 30 Nov 2026. It opens as a free limited edition on four chains at once: WAX, XRP, Base and Solana.

It is free for registered Horde members, one per person per chain. If you hold a wallet on all four chains, you can claim one on each. Paid drops for a dollar open on each chain beside it at the same time.

Not registered yet? Register first. Registration is the list.

One pool, every chain

Every piece in this gallery is one shared pool across all the chains. Every claim or sale on any chain counts against the same number, and when that number hits zero the piece closes on all of them at once. Every buy flow checks the number right before payment, so the last piece cannot sell twice.

Same price, every chain

The price is the same on every chain. It is set in XRP and converted to WAX, ETH and SOL at the live rate when you pay.

Minted when you buy it

Nothing here is pre-minted. A piece is minted only when someone buys it, on the chain they pick.

The vault

The very first mint of an edition on any chain is forever the first mint of that edition on that chain. If you ever want to move your piece from one chain to another, the original goes into the vault for safekeeping, never burned, never sold, and its twin carries on on the new chain. Move it again and the version it leaves behind is destroyed, while the new one points back to the original. So there are only ever two versions of a piece: the original, safe in the vault, and the one live on whatever chain it is on now.

Collect them all

If you hold the same edition on every chain, you earn a special reward.
